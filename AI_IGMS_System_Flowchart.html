Core End-to-End Execution Workflows

1. Online Exam & Auto-Grading Flow End-to-End

  • 1
    Teacher creates exam on CreateExamPage.jsx with questions & answer keys.
  • 2
    Frontend sends POST /api/exams. Saved in MongoDB exams collection.
  • 3
    Student opens ExamDetailsPage.jsx and submits answers via POST /api/exams/:id/submit.
  • 4
    Server gradingEngine.js compares answers against stored keys & computes percentage.
  • 5
    Result is written to submissions collection. Student & Parent view instant scorecard.

2. Roster Attendance & Marks Flow End-to-End

  • 1
    Teacher selects class/section on MarkAttendance.jsx.
  • 2
    Teacher clicks Save → POST /api/attendance payload sent to Express server.
  • 3
    Teacher inputs test scores on UploadMarks.jsx → POST /api/marks.
  • 4
    Data persisted in attendances & marks MongoDB collections.

3. Noticeboard & Leave Approval Flow End-to-End

  • 1
    Principal/Teacher broadcasts notice on Notices.jsx → POST /api/notices.
  • 2
    Teacher applies for leave on ApplyLeave.jsx → POST /api/leave.
  • 3
    Principal reviews application and updates status via PATCH /api/leave/:id/status.
  • 4
    Notice & Leave statuses updated live in MongoDB notices and leaves.
