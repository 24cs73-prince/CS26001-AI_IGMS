01 | Background & Motivation
Critical Bottlenecks in Traditional School Administration
Operational Gaps
CHALLENGE 01
Manual Paper Registers

Teachers lose over 45 minutes daily hand-recording paper attendance registers and calculating monthly percentages. Records suffer from wear, physical loss, tampering, and zero instantaneous reporting to headmasters.

CHALLENGE 02
Examination Setting Fatigue

Formulating syllabus-aligned question papers across Class 1–12 places huge cognitive burdens on teachers. Hand-evaluating 40+ answer sheets per class takes days, causing severe grading lag and human subjectivity.

CHALLENGE 03
Rural Parental Disconnect

Parents in rural public schools have zero transparency into whether their child attended school or how they scored in periodic tests, leading to undetected student absenteeism and rising dropout numbers.

09 | Security & System Reliability
Enterprise Security & Fault-Tolerant Architecture
Resilience
SHIELD 01
Stateless JWT Authentication

Cryptographically signed tokens issued upon login with userId, role, and expiration claims. Intercepted and verified on every sensitive API request.

SHIELD 02
Bcrypt Password Salting

Passwords across all 5 roles are salted and hashed using Bcrypt with 10 rounds before storage in MongoDB. Zero plain-text credentials are ever stored.

SHIELD 03
Role-Based Route Guards

Client-side React routes and Express controller endpoints verify the caller's role before processing mutations or returning confidential records.

SHIELD 04
Circuit Breaker & Fallback

Groq AI calls are bounded by a 2.5s circuit breaker with local question generation fallback, ensuring the platform never hangs during connectivity drops.
